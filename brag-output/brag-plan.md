# Brag Plan: La Perla Soft

## What is this app?
La Perla Soft is the real-time operating system for La Perla Video Bar: mesera takes orders tableside on her phone, the bar sees comandas instantly, the cashier closes out tabs — and the owner watches sales, profit, and inventory live from anywhere, without setting foot in the bar.

## The angle
The product's own visual redesign ("Marquee Nocturna") already looks like a neon bar sign — violet-night backgrounds, gold, magenta/cyan glow, a Righteous marquee wordmark. The video treats the app itself as the bar's marquee: it lights up like a sign, then proves the claim scene by scene — fast tableside ordering, instant kitchen sync, instant cash-out, and the owner's dashboard glowing with real numbers from anywhere. The whole video *is* the product's own aesthetic turned into motion — nothing generic bolted on top.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Black screen. A single gold glow ignites center-frame and the wordmark "LA PERLA SOFT" lights up letter by letter like a marquee sign powering on, each letter landing with a small glow-pulse. Immediately under it, one line slams in and holds: "Tu bar. En vivo."

## Key moments (the middle)
- Mesera's real mesas/barras grid — an occupied tile glowing cyan — tapped into the real order screen (category pills, a stepper "+" pulsing magenta, cart total ticking up), ending on a glowing "Enviar a preparación" press.
- The order lands instantly as a live comanda card in the kitchen view, gets marked "Listo" — and in the same beat the cashier's real cobro screen appears: payment-method pills, change auto-calculated, "Registrar pago y cerrar" confirms with a cash-register-style chime.
- **The non-negotiable beat**: the Admin dashboard, framed as "from anywhere" (a phone held away from the bar / a laptop at home) — real KPI tiles counting up live (Venta total, Utilidad estimada, Fiados pendientes), then a hard cut into the Reportes screen with its date-range pills and a real data table — landing on the line "Tu negocio, donde estés."

## Outro / punchline
The marquee wordmark re-ignites fully lit, steady this time (not flickering), with the tagline beneath: "Pedidos. Caja. Inventario. Todo en vivo." Logo badge glow holds for the exit.

## User flow worth showing
1. **Entry**: mesera taps an occupied/free table on the real mesas/barras grid, opens the real order screen.
2. **Key action**: she builds the order with the real category pills + stepper, sends it; it appears instantly as a kitchen comanda; cashier collects payment on the real cobro screen with live change calculation.
3. **Result**: the admin dashboard and Reportes screen update live and are reachable from anywhere — this is the emotional payoff the client explicitly wants foregrounded, not a secondary feature.

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: "a bar's own neon marquee sign coming alive" — dramatic glow-ignition reveals, big Righteous type, wide confident holds, not comedic.
- Interpretation: Big, confident motion and light — glow-ignitions and glow-pulses stand in for the "dramatic reveal" cinematic calls for. Pacing stays energetic (short holds, fast cuts between the mobile beats) but every reveal still gets a full, legible hold — dynamism comes from motion and light, not from rushing text.

## Audio direction
- Role: cinematic support with a restrained rhythmic pulse — present and driving, never dense or novelty
- Music: upbeat, warm electronic/club-adjacent bed (nightlife energy, not EDM-drop chaotic) — steady pulse from the first marquee ignition, slight lift into the admin/reports beat, settles for the outro hold
- Music treatment: starts right on the hook ignition (no pre-roll silence), stays mid-volume under scenes 2-3 so UI SFX read clearly, gentle swell under the admin/reports reveal (the emotional payoff beat), fades under the outro hold rather than hard-stopping
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset — `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app` (109.96 BPM, cues at `<brag-skill-dir>/assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.{md,json}`). Target strong cues at **8.74s** (0.99) for the Scene 2→3 cut into kitchen/cash, **13.11s** (0.98) for the Scene 3→4 hard cut into the admin/reports payoff (the most important beat-lock in the video), and **22.93s** (1.00, the single strongest cue in the whole track) for the final marquee re-ignition landing in the outro. Beat grid (0.54-0.56s spacing) available for the stepper-tap / comanda-arrival accents in scenes 2-3 — snap to every other beat for any readable text, every beat is fine for non-text glow/tick accents.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle — the marquee glow and card edge-glow may breathe gently with the music's low end; never a waveform or literal visualizer
- SFX posture: moderate, fully motion-matched — a soft glow-ignite tick per marquee letter, a light UI tap on the stepper press, a short "send" whoosh on "Enviar a preparación," a kitchen-ticket pop, a cash-register-style chime on payment confirm, a soft digital tick-up under the KPI count-up
- Audio-coupled moments: marquee letters igniting one by one (scene 1), stepper "+" tap and cart total ticking up (scene 2), comanda card arrival + "Listo" confirm + payment confirm chime (scene 3), KPI tiles counting up in sequence (scene 4)
- Restraint rule: no comedic "boing"/cartoon SFX, no chiptune stingers, no audio gag — this is a real client's commercial product, tone stays confident and premium throughout

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Marquee ignition (hook) — 3s (0.00-3.00s)
Black frame. A gold glow ignites center and "LA PERLA SOFT" lights up letter by letter (Righteous, wide tracking), each letter landing with a tiny glow-pulse. Immediately beneath, "Tu bar. En vivo." slams in and holds.
Sequential/interaction: yes — letters of the wordmark ignite one by one, left to right, each with its own glow-pop.
Audio intent: anticipation building into confident arrival — the "sign powering on" feeling.
Audio-coupled idea: a soft glow-ignite tick per letter, landing exactly on each letter's pop.
Music: bed starts right here, first strong cue on the final letter's ignition.
Transition mood: dramatic wipe → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Mesera, tableside — 5.7s (3.00-8.74s, ends on strong cue 8.74s)
Real mesas/barras grid (one tile glowing cyan "Abierta") — tap transitions into the real nuevo-pedido screen: gold category pills, a product row, the stepper "+" pulsing magenta on tap, cart total ticking up. Ends on the glowing gradient "Enviar a preparación" button pressed.
Sequential/interaction: yes — simulated tap on the occupied tile, then simulated taps on the stepper "+" (2-3 quick taps), cart total increments with each tap.
Audio intent: quick, tactile, satisfying — the feeling of speed.
Audio-coupled idea: a light UI tap per stepper press, a short "send" whoosh on the final button press.
Music: steady pulse, mid-volume.
Transition mood: clean wipe → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Kitchen + cash, in sync — 4.4s (8.74-13.11s, ends on strong cue 13.11s)
The order lands as a live comanda card in the kitchen view (card slides/pops in), gets marked "Listo" — hard cut to the real cobro screen: payment-method pills, change auto-calculating, "Registrar pago y cerrar" pressed with a confirm glow.
Sequential/interaction: yes — comanda card arrival, then its "Listo" state change, then payment pill selection and the confirm press, shown as a brisk in-order sequence.
Audio intent: instant, synchronized — two roles moving in lockstep without friction.
Audio-coupled idea: a kitchen-ticket pop on comanda arrival, a cash-register-style chime on payment confirm.
Music: pulse continues, slight build starting at the tail end into Scene 4.
Transition mood: hard cut → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Admin, anywhere (the payoff) — 6.6s (13.11-19.66s, opens on strong cue 13.11s, Reportes settle near 19.66s)
Hard cut to a wider, calmer frame: a phone/laptop silhouette held away from the bar's glow (reads as "elsewhere"). The real admin Resumen dashboard appears — KPI tiles count up in sequence (Venta total, Utilidad estimada, Fiados pendientes) — then a clean transition into the Reportes screen: date-range pills, a real data table/row. Line lands under it: "Tu negocio, donde estés."
Sequential/interaction: yes — KPI tiles count up one after another (not simultaneously), then the Reportes table rows settle in.
Audio intent: the emotional high point — reassurance and confidence, "you're not missing anything."
Audio-coupled idea: a soft digital tick-up under each KPI count, second strong music cue right on the hard cut into this scene.
Music: gentle swell, the fullest the bed gets in the whole video.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Outro / marquee re-ignited — 3.3s (19.66-23.00s, final glow lands on strong cue 22.93s)
The full wordmark "LA PERLA SOFT" holds fully lit and steady (no flicker this time), tagline beneath: "Pedidos. Caja. Inventario. Todo en vivo." Logo glow holds for the exit frame.
Sequential/interaction: none — this is the settled, confident final hold.
Audio intent: resolution — the sign stays lit, the promise is kept.
Audio-coupled idea: none; music fades under the hold rather than cutting hard.
Music: fades out across the hold.
Transition mood: soft hold → end
